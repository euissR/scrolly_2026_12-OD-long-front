The front hardens

Since Finland joined NATO in April 2023, the alliance’s border with Russia runs from the Arctic Ocean to the Baltic, and most of it passes through forest and tundra rather than past the Baltic states. Moscow responded by reorganising its military districts – and says openly that this is aimed at NATO.

At the southern end of the front, the logic changes.
